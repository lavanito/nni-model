# Eurostat: hours worked and persons employed (cross-check only)

`nama_10_a10_e_emp_total.csv`: Eurostat dataset `nama_10_a10_e` ("Employment by main industry (NACE Rev.2) - national accounts - annual data"), all industries (`TOTAL`), domestic concept (`EMP_DC`), for the 21 euro area members, 2000–2025. Series: `THS_HW` (thousand hours worked) and `THS_PER` (thousand persons). Economy codes are Eurostat's (Greece is `EL`).

Downloaded on 26 October 2026 through the Eurostat dissemination API, in the browser; dataset last updated 21 October 2026.

Use: cross-check of the euro area hours built from PWT (`nni_model/crosschecks.py`). Not a model input.
