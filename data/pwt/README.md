# Penn World Table 11.0: hours and employment

`pwt110_hours.csv` holds, for the six economies and the 21 euro area members, 2000–2023: persons engaged (`emp`, millions), average annual hours worked per person engaged (`avh`), population (`pop`, millions) and output-side real GDP (`rgdpo`, cross-check only). Extracted on 26 October 2026 from `pwt110.xlsx` (sheet "Data"), attached by Lavan.

Licence: CC BY 4.0. Cite Feenstra, Inklaar and Timmer (2015), "The Next Generation of the Penn World Table", American Economic Review 105(10), 3150–3182, and the PWT 11.0 DOI 10.34894/FABVLR.

PWT's hours come from the Conference Board's Total Economy Database, which fills gaps with ILO modelled estimates and its own estimates (A2 data list). India's employment changes source in 2019 (ETD to ILO official).
